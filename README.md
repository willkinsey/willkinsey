# Will Kinsey

## Healthcare Technology, Strategic Initiatives, and Applied AI

I’m a healthcare technology leader with roughly 20 years in medical equipment. My primary work is leading strategic initiatives, major project delivery, and organizational change. Product development supports that broader work, helping turn priorities into coordinated outcomes.

My work connects executive decision support, capital planning, equipment lifecycle analysis, and analytics with the execution of strategic priorities. These disciplines connect long-range choices with practical action: clarifying the decision, bringing relevant information into view, and helping cross-functional teams stay aligned as work progresses. My approach combines business judgment, structured problem-solving, and collaboration across functions.

I also apply AI to knowledge work through workflow automation and agent workflows. I’m interested in practical ways these tools can support clearer collaboration and stronger work, while keeping people’s goals and judgment at the center. I share reusable resources and education so others can adapt these methods to their own work.

## Projects
